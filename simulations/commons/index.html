What to notice

Compare resource visibility with communication. Each agent has a different appetite and willingness to restrain harvesting. Watch whether restraint arrives before the resource is exhausted. Monitoring uses an imperfect estimate of regrowth, so a cap can still be too generous. Change pressure mid-run to test recovery.

Why it matters

Cooperation depends on feedback, incentives and renewal, not goodwill alone. Regrowth slows near depletion. Different rules can produce recovery, fluctuating use or collapse; none guarantees success. Complete depletion needs a reset to recover. These are exploratory behavioural rules, not a forecast of any particular commons.
