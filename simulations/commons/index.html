What to notice

Boats share one lake, but their catches differ. Fish regenerate while fishers harvest. Compare the abundant and fragile lakes, then change pressure mid-run. In Communication helps?, turn sharing off and compare from Reset. Information helps fishers adjust; it cannot create fish faster than the lake renews them.

Why it matters

A commons depends on renewal, incentives and timely feedback. Restraint can arrive too late. The rules permit recovery, sustained use and collapse; cooperation does not guarantee success. Once every fish is gone, this simplified lake needs a reset.
