Why can sensible corrections make a system less stable?

Negative stock means unmet demand. Changing delay restarts the pipeline; other settings change live. Orders are bounded at 80 units per round.

What to notice

Press Play, then add a small demand rise. The automatic manager corrects the stock shortfall without accounting for orders still travelling. Raise response strength or delay and watch corrections stack up. In manual mode, pause and place one order before each Step. The pipeline reveals what has yet to arrive.

Why it matters

A sensible response to today’s shortage can become tomorrow’s surplus. Delays turn feedback into over-correction. Slower adjustment can help, but too little response leaves persistent shortages. This compact stock model is an experiment with timing, not a complete supply-chain game.
