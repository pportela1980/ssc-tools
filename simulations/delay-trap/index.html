What to notice

The tap changes the water entering the pipe, not the water already at the shower. Watch the coloured water travel. In Overcorrecting, the controller reacts again before its earlier change arrives. Switch to You to try the tap yourself; keep it steady long enough to feel its effect.

Why it matters

Feedback arrives late in many systems. Strong corrections made while earlier actions are still travelling can swing the result from one extreme to another. Patience reduces that tendency. This is an illustrative temperature model, not guidance for operating a real shower.
