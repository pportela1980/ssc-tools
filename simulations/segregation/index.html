What to notice

Start with the mixed neighbourhood. Ringed homes are households whose local preference is unmet. Each round, they move to available homes; the highlighted paths follow a few of those moves. Compare Integrated with Mild preference. Notice how patches form even though residents do not demand complete separation.

Why it matters

Individual moves change the neighbourhood for everyone else. Nobody plans the final pattern, but local choices reinforce one another. This is a simplified mechanism for exploring segregation, not a complete explanation of real housing patterns.
