What to notice

Start with a modest preference, then press Play. Large patches can emerge even when nobody demands complete separation. Try changing the radius or the balance between populations. Satisfaction measures local comfort; “unlike neighbours” counts contacts across categories, so its baseline also depends on the population mix.

Why it matters

Local choices reshape the neighbourhoods that shape the next choices. A global pattern needs no planner. This simplified model explores a mechanism; it does not explain every cause of real segregation.
