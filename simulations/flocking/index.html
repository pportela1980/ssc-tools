What to notice

Press Play and watch the trails begin to point together. Switch Match direction off, or reduce how far each bird can see. Keeping space avoids crowding; staying together attracts neighbours. Move your pointer through the flock to create a local disturbance. Different balances produce different kinds of motion.

Why it matters

No bird sees the whole flock or issues a plan. Coordination grows from local responses. A neat formation is not automatically better; the experiment shows how order can emerge, not how human groups should be led.
